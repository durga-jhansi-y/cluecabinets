WRITTEN WITNESS STATEMENT
Case ID: CASE-001
Witness: Rosa Delgado, female, 52 (owner/operator, Taco Loco Stand)
Taken by: Officer L. Sandoval (#4821)
Date/Time taken: 00:41 AM

STATEMENT:
I close the stand around 11:40 PM most nights. Around 11:30 a man came up and
ordered two tacos and a soda. He paid cash and took a receipt. He was wearing a
dark hooded sweatshirt, hood up at first. When he pulled out his wallet I saw a
tattoo on his left forearm, I think a flower, maybe a rose. He was nervous and
kept looking toward the bridge. He had a gray backpack. He was maybe in his
late 20s, medium build, not very tall.

After I closed up I walked to my truck. I saw an older sedan pulling out of Lot A
fast. It was dark, I would say dark green or dark blue, hard to tell under those
lights. I did not get a plate.

I did not see anyone get robbed. I did not see the man with the watch.
